# input-test's gestures (ADR-0036, step b): the one source for tools/android-reference.sh, which plays them
# through the emulator's console as its touchscreen's reports, and for runtime/tests/input_test.cpp, which
# posts them with apkrt_input_post. One motion event per line, as Android's reader makes one of each report:
#   <delay in ms before it> <action> <id>:<x>,<y> ... [| <id>:<x>,<y> ...]
# The action is DOWN, UP, MOVE, POINTER_DOWN(<index>) or POINTER_UP(<index>). The pointers follow in the
# order of their IDs, with places in fractions of the screen's width and height; a DOWN or POINTER_DOWN lists
# them with the new one, an UP or POINTER_UP with the lifted one. A MOVE may have several samples, oldest
# first, separated by "|": the emulator gets them as reports in one burst, the Mac as one event with history.
# The gestures come in the order input-test names them. MOVEs are 40 ms apart, more than the 20 ms up to
# which Android extrapolates a touch at the frame, so that no resampled time lies after a later event's.
